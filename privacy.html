Privacy policy

This policy explains how Brand.UP handles the data of people who apply through this website.

Data controller

The Octanbro’s S.àr.Ls (Brand.UP), 233, rue Pierre Gansen, L-4570 Niederkorn, Luxembourg — RCS B277863. Contact: hello@brandup.com.

Data we collect

  • Step 1: first name, last name, email address.
  • Application form: contact details, country and city, languages, areas of expertise, experience, tools, portfolio and attached files, rates, availability, and your answers to the open questions.

Why we use it

Only to review your application, contact you about a freelance collaboration and, if relevant, prepare your onboarding. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, and your consent, given by ticking the box in the form.

Who can access it

The Brand.UP founders and, where needed, the assistant in charge of following up applications. The technical tools used are Tally (forms) and Notion (application storage). Your data is never sold or shared with third parties for commercial purposes.

How long we keep it

2 years from our last exchange, after which it is deleted. You can ask us to delete it earlier at any time.

Third-party content

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and, on the presentation page, a video hosted by YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) or Vimeo. These services may receive your IP address.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data, object to or restrict its processing, request its portability and withdraw your consent. Write to hello@brandup.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the Luxembourg data protection authority (CNPD — Commission nationale pour la protection des données).
